<!-- ABOUT ME SECTION -->
## 💼 About Me

I'm currently working as a full-stack developer, where I get to wear multiple hats—from architecting scalable backend services to crafting smooth, responsive user interfaces. My journey in software development has taught me that the best solutions come from understanding both the technical constraints and the human experience. Whether I'm optimizing database queries or fine-tuning animations, I'm always thinking about the end user.

My development philosophy is simple: write code that's maintainable, scalable, and—most importantly—understandable. I believe in the power of clean architecture and well-documented code. There's something deeply satisfying about refactoring a complex system into something elegant and straightforward. I'm a strong advocate for TypeScript's type safety, Flutter's cross-platform capabilities, and the robustness that comes from proper testing.

What drives me is the intersection of technology and creativity. I love the challenge of taking an abstract idea and transforming it into a tangible product that people actually enjoy using. There's a unique thrill in seeing users interact with something you've built, especially when they don't even notice the complexity you've hidden behind a simple interface. That's the sweet spot—when technology becomes invisible and the experience becomes everything.

I'm particularly passionate about mobile development and modern web frameworks. The mobile-first world we live in demands applications that are not just functional but feel native and intuitive. I've spent considerable time mastering Flutter because it allows me to build beautiful, performant apps for multiple platforms without compromising on quality. On the web side, I gravitate toward Angular and React for their component-based architectures, and NestJS for building robust, enterprise-grade backends.

### [State of Matter](https://github.com/dev-akshat/state-of-matter)
A physics simulation application that visualizes the behavior of matter in different states. This project challenged me to optimize rendering performance while maintaining accurate physics calculations, resulting in smooth 60fps animations even with hundreds of particles. Built with modern web technologies to make complex scientific concepts accessible and interactive.

**Tech Stack:** JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, Physics Engine  
🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/dev-akshat/state-of-matter)

---

### [HackerNews Client](https://github.com/dev-akshat/HackerNews)
A clean, fast HackerNews reader that focuses on user experience and performance. I implemented intelligent caching strategies and lazy loading to minimize API calls and ensure instant navigation. The app features a minimalist design that puts content first, with dark mode support and customizable reading preferences.

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, HackerNews API  
🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/dev-akshat/HackerNews)
